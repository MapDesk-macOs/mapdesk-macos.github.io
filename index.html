MapDesk

Manage your contacts and deals visually on a map. Geocoded addresses turn into pins you can explore, filter, and organize. A built-in deal pipeline tracks stages, value, and progress—all in one native macOS app.

Visual Network Overview

Pins on the map reveal your contacts and opportunities by location. Filter by tags, deals, or activity to focus on what matters. No more scattered notes or spreadsheets—everything stays spatially organized.

Deal Pipeline

Track deals through customizable stages with clear visibility into value and progress. Log activities, attach notes, and review pipelines without switching apps. Focused inspectors provide detail on demand.

Seamless Apple Integration

Import from Apple Contacts on your terms. MapDesk keeps its data separate until you choose to sync back. Perfect for individuals and small teams in the Apple ecosystem.

Route Planning

String together visits with intelligent routing. See your week's territory at a glance—optimized for when work takes you on the road.

Native macOS Experience

Fast, focused, and built for macOS. Feels like a natural desk companion for managing people, places, and opportunities.

How It Works

1. Import Contacts
Bring in data from Apple Contacts. Geocode addresses to create map pins automatically.

2. Organize and Filter
Tag contacts, assign deals to pipeline stages, and filter the map view by criteria like location or status.

3. Track Progress
Log activities, monitor deal values, and use inspectors for deep dives—all without leaving the map.

4. Plan Routes
Build efficient visit sequences. Export or review your territory plans directly in the app.

FAQ

Yes, it imports on your schedule and keeps MapDesk data separate until you export changes back.

Can I use it for teams?
Designed for individuals and small teams. Share maps or export data as needed.

Is route planning built-in?
Yes, string visits together with optimized paths visible right on the map.

What about privacy?
All data stays local on your Mac. No cloud required.
